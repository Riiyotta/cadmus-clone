# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-27)

First build of the full design-repo (Situation A) on top of a stub that held only the templates, routes and the measurement script. Status `design-review-pending`; agent attestation, not a human sign-off.

- Tokens derived by script from `tailwind.config.cjs`, `src/index.css`, the motion constants and the spacing/width utilities actually used (no invented values); catalog and policy regenerated and checked.
- 16 primitives, 14 components, 82 section contracts with provenance (`source` {id, file, tag, lines}, tag line pinned by hash) separate from taxonomy (`semantic` {id, category}), `retrievalEligible`, responsive evidence and measured heights at 1440/768/390.
- Re-derived all 181 routes from the router and data files; templates split by semantic purpose; measured every route at 1440px with 0 structural mismatches. Stub deltas: `article.hero` and `pricing.plans-hero` are classed HERO; the whole-body article/policy sections are decomposed (`article.body-chapter`, `article.policy-section`) with a reference-only `article.original-link`; empty article bodies are allowed in reference mode.
- PageSpec schema (generated by `schema/build_schema.py`) with required `mode`; semantic validator with `GENERATED_MODE_*` rules and normalisation; source-brand registry derived by `extraction/build_source_brand.py`; asset roles with three-way availability pinned in code; generation-safe example per template; reference examples per route family.
- Wrapper-height gate wired into `verify_all`; `verify_all`, adversarial suite (controls and mutations) and `prove_drift` (59 cases) all pass; baseline audit passes.
- `ia/` updated to the same section ids and decomposition and regenerated.
- Known limits: no visual-parity measurement; string-based detection; no human review (see README).
